# Skeleton

A placeholder shape while content loads.

## What you provide

`variant` text | circle | rect; `width`, `height`.

## Look

- `surface-active`, pulsing; `radius-sm` for text lines, `radius-lg` for rects.

## Do / don't

- Mirror the layout that will appear, three to five shapes at most. Beyond ~2 s show MarkdropLoader instead.
