# ImmersiveExit

The one control over a full-bleed document: a faded pill that returns to the framed view.

## What you provide

`onExit`, `placement` bottom-right | top-right (video).

## Look

- black/60 pill with white/85 text at 45% opacity; full on hover/focus; shows an Esc key.

## Do / don't

- Shown to every reader, not only owners.
