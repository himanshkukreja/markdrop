# AmbientBackground

The marketing backdrop: two drifting accent glows and a dotted grid fading toward the edges.

## What you provide

No props. Fixed, `z-index: -10`; give the content above it `isolation: isolate`.

## Look

- Glows use `accent-ring` / `accent-soft`, so they tint correctly in both themes. Pair with `.md-gradient-text` on one hero word.

## Do / don't

- Landing and marketing pages only.
