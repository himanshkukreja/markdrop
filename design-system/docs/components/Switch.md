# Switch

An on/off control that applies immediately.

## What you provide

`label`, `checked`, `onChange`, `disabled`. Rendered as `role="switch"`.

## Look

- 36×20 track on `border-strong`; on = `accent` with a white 16px thumb and `shadow-sm`.

## Do / don't

- Only for settings that take effect at once with no Save button; otherwise use Checkbox inside the form.
