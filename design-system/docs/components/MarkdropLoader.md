# MarkdropLoader

The branded loading state: the logo's drop falls into the mark and ripples, over the wordmark and a sweep.

## What you provide

`label` (null hides), `size` sm | md | lg, `hideWordmark`.

## Look

- Mark geometry identical to MarkdropMark; `shadow-glow` halo; sweep bar in `accent-text` on `surface-active`.
- Reduced motion parks the drop mid-fall.

## Do / don't

- Page and document loads only — never inside controls.
