## Using Markdrop

::::cards
:::card[Publish documents]{href="/documents/writing" icon="lines"}
Paste Markdown and get a page with diagrams, math and highlighted code, at a link you can name and keep editing. (1:31)
:::
:::card[Files that render]{href="/files/supported-types" icon="upload"}
Upload an HTML page, PDF, spreadsheet, Word document, video or zipped site, and get a link that opens it in the browser. (1:26)
:::
:::card[Share with control]{href="/sharing/who-can-open" icon="lock"}
Anyone with the link, your team, or only people you add, plus passwords, expiry and access requests. (1:16)
:::
:::card[End-to-end encryption]{href="/sharing/end-to-end-encryption" icon="key"}
Encrypt a document in your browser. The key stays in the link, and our servers never receive it. (1:18)
:::
:::card[Workspaces and domains]{href="/workspaces/create" icon="globe"}
Shared folders and roles for your team, served from your own domain with your logo and colours. (1:16)
:::
:::card[Creator analytics]{href="/analytics/overview" icon="chart"}
How far people read, which PDF pages they reach, and where viewers stop watching. Patterns, never who. (1:28)
:::
:::card[VS Code sync]{href="/vscode/sync" icon="edit"}
Publish from VS Code, and every save updates the link. Web edits come back into your file. (1:20)
:::
:::card[AI apps and the CLI]{href="/ai/overview" icon="zap"}
Let Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor publish and update links, and upload big files from the terminal. (1:29)
:::
:::card[Send files peer to peer]{href="/share/send-files" icon="wave"}
Send files straight to another device, encrypted, with nothing stored on a server. (1:23)
:::
:::card[README builder]{href="/documents/readme-builder" icon="builder"}
Build a README from 49 ready-made sections, diagrams included, then copy, download or publish it. (1:12)
:::
