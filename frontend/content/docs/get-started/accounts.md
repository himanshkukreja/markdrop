Writing and publishing a document never needs an account, and neither does reading anything shared with "Anyone with the link". An account is for the things that have to belong to someone: files you upload, documents only certain people can open, and analytics about your readers.

## Without an account

You can:

- Publish documents at [markdrop.in/new](https://markdrop.in/new), with a custom link, a password, an expiry, and end-to-end encryption.
- Publish a README from the [README builder](/documents/readme-builder).
- Edit or delete what you published, using its [edit secret](/documents/edit-key).
- Send files peer to peer from [markdrop.in/share](https://markdrop.in/share). See [Send files peer to peer](/share/send-files).
- Read anything open to anyone with the link.

Everything you publish without an account is open to anyone with the link. "Only people added" needs an owner to add the people, so it needs an account.

## What an account adds

| With an account you can | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Upload files: HTML, PDF, sheets, Word, images, video, zipped sites | [Publish your first file](/get-started/first-file) |
| Keep everything you publish in one list | **My files** |
| Limit a document to people you add, and add them by email | [Share with specific people](/sharing/share-with-people) |
| Open documents shared with your address, and ask for access | **Shared with me**, [Request access](/sharing/request-access) |
| See views, read depth and video watch time | [Analytics](/analytics/overview) |
| Create workspaces, folders and custom domains | [Create a workspace](/workspaces/create) |
| Publish from VS Code, the CLI and AI assistants | [API tokens](/account/api-tokens), [AI and MCP](/ai/overview) |
| Save a copy of someone else's document | **More actions › Save a copy** |

## Sign in

There is no password to remember. Go to [markdrop.in/login](https://markdrop.in/login) and choose one:

- **Continue with Google.**
- **Email me a code.** Markdrop emails you a 6-digit code and a sign-in link. Enter the code, or open the link on the same device.

![The "Sign in to Markdrop" page with a Continue with Google button, an Email field and an Email me a code button, beside a list of what signing in adds.](/docs-assets/get-started/accounts/sign-in-options.webp)

Your first sign-in creates the account. You stay signed in on that browser for 30 days, or until you choose **Sign out**.

:::note
Sign in with the address people share things with. Markdrop matches shared documents to the email address of the account you're signed in with. Letter case doesn't matter, but `alice+docs@acme.com` and `alice@acme.com` are different addresses.
:::

## Move anonymous documents into your account

A document you published while signed out isn't attached to any account. To attach it, sign in in the same tab you published from and choose **Save to my account** on the document. See [Edit with your edit secret](/documents/edit-key#save-it-to-your-account).

Once it's yours, it appears in **My files** with its analytics, and you can choose who can open it.

## Your profile

Open **Settings** in the sidebar to change your **Display name**, which people in your workspaces see. Your email is your sign-in address and can't be changed there.
