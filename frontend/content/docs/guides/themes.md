A theme sets the whole guide's layout, fonts, colours and corners. Pick one in the guide's **Design** tab; you can switch any time, and nothing about your pages changes.

![The Design tab: under Theme, the filter (All 14, Docs, Help, Developer, New 8) and the theme cards, each with a small drawing of it, with Clean selected; on the right a live preview of Acme's Quickstart page in the Clean theme with Acme's logo and teal colour.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/design-panel.webp)

## Switch themes

::::steps
### Open Design
Open the guide and choose **Design**. Only Guides admins can change it; everyone else can look around.

### Try the themes
Choose a theme under **Theme**. The **Preview** updates at once. Use **Light** and **Dark** above the preview to see both. To narrow the list, choose **Docs** (product docs), **Help** (help centers), **Developer** or **New** above the themes.

### Save it
Choose **Save design**, or **Discard changes** to go back to what's saved. Readers see the new design after the next [publish](/guides/publishing).
::::

![Theme filtered to New: the eight newer themes as cards, Midnight, Paper, Terminal, Corporate, Studio, Academic, Atlas and Pastel, each marked New with a line about what it's for.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/design-theme-filter.webp)

Once it's saved, a banner says **Design saved**, with **Preview the guide** and **Publish now**, so you can put the new look live straight away.

![The Design saved banner under the guide's bar: "Readers see the new look after you publish. It takes about a minute.", with Preview the guide and Publish now.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/design-saved.webp)

## The fourteen themes

Every theme has a light and a dark palette, and text, links and buttons stay readable in both. Any of them can take your brand colour and logo. The pictures show the same Acme pages, published in each theme with no brand colours.

### Clean

A sidebar layout. Calm and familiar: a page tree on the left, the page, and its contents on the right. The Markdrop docs use it.

![Acme's Quickstart page in the Clean theme: sidebar on the left, the page in the middle, On this page and page actions on the right.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/theme-clean.webp)

### Editorial

Serif headings and a centred, narrower reading column. Sections are links under the header instead of a sidebar.

![Quickstart in the Editorial theme: serif headings, warm paper colours, section links under the header and a centred column.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/theme-editorial.webp)

### Technical

Dense and API-first: a smaller type size, sharp corners, monospaced labels and dark code blocks even in the light theme.

![Quickstart in the Technical theme: compact text, monospaced group labels and a dark code block.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/theme-technical.webp)

### Minimal

No sidebar chrome and lots of air: sections under the header, one centred column, no divider lines.

![Quickstart in the Minimal theme: a plain header with section links and generous spacing.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/theme-minimal.webp)

### Playful

Round and bold: rounded buttons and search box, heavier navigation, cards that lift on hover.

![Quickstart in the Playful theme: rounded shapes and a violet accent.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/theme-playful.webp)

### Help center

Search first, then categories: the home page is a search box over cards for each group, and articles show the other articles in their category.

![The home page in the Help center theme: a search hero with suggested questions, and Browse by category cards for Getting started, Guides, Reference and Help.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/theme-helpcenter.webp)

### Midnight

For developer tools: deep navy grounds, a violet accent and a crisp sans-serif. The current page is marked with a line instead of a highlight. Its dark palette is the one it's designed around; pick **Dark only** under [Colour mode](/guides/brand-and-style#fonts-colour-mode-and-corners) to keep it there.

![Quickstart in the Midnight theme, as a first-time reader sees it: deep navy grounds, a violet accent, a violet line beside the current page in the sidebar and a violet note.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/theme-midnight.webp)

### Paper

For handbooks: warm off-white paper, serif text at a book's size, and a quieter sidebar without icons or highlights.

![Quickstart in the Paper theme: warm off-white paper, serif headings and text at a book's size, and a plain sidebar with a rust-red current page.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/theme-paper.webp)

### Terminal

For command-line tools: monospaced headings, navigation and labels, a green accent, square corners and a `$` prompt before each page title. It reads best in dark.

![Quickstart in the Terminal theme, in dark: monospaced headings and sidebar, a green accent, square corners and a $ prompt before the page title.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/theme-terminal.webp)

### Corporate

For enterprise products: strict and compact, with smaller type, tighter spacing, a blue-grey palette and nearly square corners.

![Quickstart in the Corporate theme: compact type and spacing, a blue-grey palette, a highlighted current page and nearly square corners.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/theme-corporate.webp)

### Studio

For product launches: big, bold display headings, large rounded cards with a lift, a rose accent and round buttons.

![Quickstart in the Studio theme: big, bold headings, a rose accent and round buttons.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/theme-studio.webp)

### Academic

For knowledge bases: serif text, numbered sections (1., 1.1) and smaller, footnote-style notes, metadata and callouts, with a green accent.

![Quickstart in the Academic theme: serif text, numbered sections (1. Before you start, 2. Install the CLI, 3. Create a project) and a green accent.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/theme-academic.webp)

### Atlas

For big help centers: the home page is a search box over a grid of category cards, the sections in the header are tabs, and articles keep the sidebar so readers can move around a large guide.

![The home page in the Atlas theme: the sections Getting started, Guides, Reference and Help as tabs in the header, a search box with suggested questions under it in a blue-tinted hero, and Browse by category cards for Getting started, Guides, Reference and Help with their articles.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/theme-atlas.webp)

### Pastel

For education and communities: soft pink-tinted grounds and cards, rounded buttons, search and navigation, and friendly headings.

![Quickstart in the Pastel theme: soft pink-tinted grounds, a rounded search box and buttons, and a pink current page.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/theme-pastel.webp)

## Customize a theme

When a theme is close but not quite right, make it your own. In **Design**, under **Custom theme**, choose **Customize**. The theme builder opens full screen: the controls on the left, and on the right a large live preview of your guide's pages, drawn with the same styles your readers get.

Above the preview, switch between **Home** and **Article**, **Light** and **Dark**, and desktop and phone width. The article has a callout of each kind, code, a table and cards, so you can see every change where it matters. Everything updates as you change it.

::::steps
### Pick a starting point
**Start from** (at the top) is the theme your design builds on. Its layout, fonts and colours are the defaults for everything you don't change. You can switch it at any time; your changes stay.

### Change what you like
The controls are grouped. Each group has **Reset** to put that group back to the theme's own.

### Save your design
Choose **Save design**. You're back in **Design**, with the **Design saved** banner and **Publish now**. Readers see the new look after you publish.
::::

**Undo** and **Redo** at the top step back and forward through your changes (or press ⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z; Ctrl on Windows). Closing the builder with the back arrow keeps your changes in the design without saving them; **Save design** in Design saves them later, and **Discard changes** there drops them.

| Group | What you can change |
| --- | --- |
| **Colours** | For light and dark separately: page background, cards and panels, sidebar, header, borders, text, muted text, the accent (links and buttons), text selection, code background and text, and the note, tip, warning and danger callouts. Also the colour mode (both, light only or dark only). |
| **Typography** | The heading, body and code fonts, the text size, line height, heading weight and the width of the article column. |
| **Shape** | Corner radius (none to extra large), buttons and the search box (square, rounded or pill) and cards (flat, bordered or raised). |
| **Layout** | The header (left or centred, solid or transparent, the sections as header tabs or not), the tabs' style (pills or underline), the sidebar (pills, lines or plain, with or without page icons), callouts (tinted, bordered or a bar down the left), code blocks (match the page, always light or always dark) and links in text (underlined, underlined on hover, or bold). |
| **Icons** | One style for every icon in the guide: **Regular**, **Light** (a finer stroke), **Bold** (a heavier one) or **Duotone** (shapes washed with their own colour). |
| **Advanced** | Custom CSS, for anything the controls don't cover. See [Custom CSS](/guides/brand-and-style#custom-css). |

A setting you haven't changed says **Theme default** and follows the theme you started from. To clear one colour, choose the **×** beside it. **Back to** the theme's name, under **Custom theme** in Design, drops every customization at once.

### Colours that stay readable

Each colour has a swatch (the colour picker), its hex value and, in browsers that have one, an eyedropper to pick a colour from anywhere on your screen. Under **Suggested palettes**, choose a ready-made set of light and dark colours, or one built from your logo's colours when [Match my brand](/guides/brand-and-style#match-my-brand) is on.

The builder checks every pair readers read against the WCAG AA contrast level (4.5:1): text on the page, cards, header and sidebar, muted text, links, button labels, code, each callout and selected text. When a colour you picked falls short, it says which pair and by how much. Choose **Fix** beside it, or **Fix all**, to switch to the nearest colour that passes. Your colour is never changed without you asking, but a published guide never shows unreadable text either: until you fix it, readers get the nearest readable shade.

## Your templates

Save a design to use it again, on this guide or any other guide in the workspace. Templates keep the theme, colours, fonts, shape, layout, icons and custom CSS, but not a guide's logo or brand colours, so each guide keeps its own.

- **Save as template** is in the theme builder's top bar. In Design, **Save current** under **Your templates** does the same.
- **Your templates** in Design lists the workspace's templates, beside the themes. Choose one to load it into the design, then **Save design**. (A guide in your personal space has your personal templates.)
- The **⋯** menu on a template has **Rename**, **Duplicate**, **Update with current design**, **Export as JSON** and **Delete**. **Import** (beside **Save current**) adds a template from an exported file, for example one from another workspace.

Guides admins of a workspace save and change its templates; everyone who can open a guide's Design can see them. A workspace can have up to 50 templates. Deleting a template doesn't change guides that use its design.

## Reset the design

To start over, put the design back to the one your guide started with: the design it had right after a [GitBook import](/guides/import-from-gitbook) (the look and brand the import brought), or the one it was created with (a template's theme, or Clean).

1. In **Design**, scroll to **Reset design** at the bottom and choose **Reset design**.
2. The dialog lists what changes, for example the theme, brand colours, fonts or custom theme. Choose **Reset design** to confirm.

The reset is saved like **Save design**: readers see it after you [publish](/guides/publishing), and the **Publish** screen counts it as a change. Right after, **Undo** in the message at the bottom of the screen puts back the design you had. Your [templates](#your-templates) stay, and logos you uploaded stay in the guide's files. Pages, navigation, domains, people and settings outside Design don't change.

**Reset design** is greyed out when the design already is the starting one. A guide made before resets were recorded goes back to the best match: its design if it was never changed, a rebuilt import look (Clean with the logo and colour the import brought), or else the default design. The dialog says which.

![The Reset the design? dialog: "This puts back the design Acme had when it was created", then What changes: Theme Midnight → Clean, Match my brand turned off, Brand colours, Fonts (headings Fraunces → Theme default) and Custom theme removed; a line that saved design templates are kept and nothing changes for readers until you publish; Cancel and Reset design.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/design-reset.webp)

## Make it yours

On top of a theme you can add your logo and colours, change the fonts, colour mode and corners, and add custom CSS. See [Brand and style](/guides/brand-and-style). For everything else, [customize the theme](#customize-a-theme).
