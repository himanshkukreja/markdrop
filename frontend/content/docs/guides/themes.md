A theme sets the whole guide's layout, fonts, colours and corners. Pick one in the guide's **Design** tab; you can switch any time, and nothing about your pages changes.

![The Design tab: under Theme, the filter (All 14, Docs, Help, Developer, New 8) and the theme cards, each with a small drawing of it, with Clean selected; on the right a live preview of Acme's Quickstart page in the Clean theme with Acme's logo and teal colour.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/design-panel.webp)

## Switch themes

::::steps
### Open Design
Open the guide and choose **Design**. Only Guides admins can change it; everyone else can look around.

### Try the themes
Choose a theme under **Theme**. The **Preview** updates at once. Use **Light** and **Dark** above the preview to see both. To narrow the list, choose **Docs** (product docs), **Help** (help centers), **Developer** or **New** above the themes.

### Save it
Choose **Save design**, or **Reset** to go back to what's saved. Readers see the new design after the next [publish](/guides/publishing).
::::

![Theme filtered to New: the eight newer themes as cards, Midnight, Paper, Terminal, Corporate, Studio, Academic, Atlas and Pastel, each marked New with a line about what it's for.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/design-theme-filter.webp)

Once it's saved, a banner says **Design saved**, with **Preview the guide** and **Publish now**, so you can put the new look live straight away.

![The Design saved banner under the guide's bar: "Readers see the new look after you publish. It takes about a minute.", with Preview the guide and Publish now.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/design-saved.webp)

## The fourteen themes

Every theme has a light and a dark palette, and text, links and buttons stay readable in both. Any of them can take your brand colour and logo. The pictures show the same Acme pages, published in each theme with no brand colours.

### Clean

A sidebar layout. Calm and familiar: a page tree on the left, the page, and its contents on the right. The Markdrop docs use it.

![Acme's Quickstart page in the Clean theme: sidebar on the left, the page in the middle, On this page and page actions on the right.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/theme-clean.webp)

### Editorial

Serif headings and a centred, narrower reading column. Sections are links under the header instead of a sidebar.

![Quickstart in the Editorial theme: serif headings, warm paper colours, section links under the header and a centred column.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/theme-editorial.webp)

### Technical

Dense and API-first: a smaller type size, sharp corners, monospaced labels and dark code blocks even in the light theme.

![Quickstart in the Technical theme: compact text, monospaced group labels and a dark code block.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/theme-technical.webp)

### Minimal

No sidebar chrome and lots of air: sections under the header, one centred column, no divider lines.

![Quickstart in the Minimal theme: a plain header with section links and generous spacing.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/theme-minimal.webp)

### Playful

Round and bold: rounded buttons and search box, heavier navigation, cards that lift on hover.

![Quickstart in the Playful theme: rounded shapes and a violet accent.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/theme-playful.webp)

### Help center

Search first, then categories: the home page is a search box over cards for each group, and articles show the other articles in their category.

![The home page in the Help center theme: a search hero with suggested questions, and Browse by category cards for Getting started, Guides, Reference and Help.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/theme-helpcenter.webp)

### Midnight

For developer tools: deep navy grounds, a violet accent and a crisp sans-serif. The current page is marked with a line instead of a highlight. Its dark palette is the one it's designed around; pick **Dark only** under [Colour mode](/guides/brand-and-style#fonts-colour-mode-and-corners) to keep it there.

![Quickstart in the Midnight theme, as a first-time reader sees it: deep navy grounds, a violet accent, a violet line beside the current page in the sidebar and a violet note.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/theme-midnight.webp)

### Paper

For handbooks: warm off-white paper, serif text at a book's size, and a quieter sidebar without icons or highlights.

![Quickstart in the Paper theme: warm off-white paper, serif headings and text at a book's size, and a plain sidebar with a rust-red current page.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/theme-paper.webp)

### Terminal

For command-line tools: monospaced headings, navigation and labels, a green accent, square corners and a `$` prompt before each page title. It reads best in dark.

![Quickstart in the Terminal theme, in dark: monospaced headings and sidebar, a green accent, square corners and a $ prompt before the page title.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/theme-terminal.webp)

### Corporate

For enterprise products: strict and compact, with smaller type, tighter spacing, a blue-grey palette and nearly square corners.

![Quickstart in the Corporate theme: compact type and spacing, a blue-grey palette, a highlighted current page and nearly square corners.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/theme-corporate.webp)

### Studio

For product launches: big, bold display headings, large rounded cards with a lift, a rose accent and round buttons.

![Quickstart in the Studio theme: big, bold headings, a rose accent and round buttons.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/theme-studio.webp)

### Academic

For knowledge bases: serif text, numbered sections (1., 1.1) and smaller, footnote-style notes, metadata and callouts, with a green accent.

![Quickstart in the Academic theme: serif text, numbered sections (1. Before you start, 2. Install the CLI, 3. Create a project) and a green accent.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/theme-academic.webp)

### Atlas

For big help centers: the home page is a search box over a grid of category cards, the sections in the header are tabs, and articles keep the sidebar so readers can move around a large guide.

![The home page in the Atlas theme: a search box with suggested questions in a blue-tinted hero, and Browse by category cards for Getting started, Guides, Reference and Help with their articles.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/theme-atlas.webp)

### Pastel

For education and communities: soft pink-tinted grounds and cards, rounded buttons, search and navigation, and friendly headings.

![Quickstart in the Pastel theme: soft pink-tinted grounds, a rounded search box and buttons, and a pink current page.](/docs-assets/guides/themes/theme-pastel.webp)

## Make it yours

On top of a theme you can add your logo and colours, change the fonts, colour mode and corners, and add custom CSS. See [Brand and style](/guides/brand-and-style).
