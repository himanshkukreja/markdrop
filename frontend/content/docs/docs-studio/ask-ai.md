Ask AI lets readers ask a question in their own words and get an answer written from your docs, with a link to the exact section each part came from. It only uses your **published** pages: drafts never reach it.

::clip{src="/docs-assets/docs-studio/ask-ai/reader-ask-ai" alt="On Acme's published Connect Postgres page, opening Ask Acme, asking how to connect a Postgres database, the answer streaming in with numbered sources, and choosing source 1 to jump to the Create a read-only user section."}

## Turn it on

::::steps
### Open the setting
Open the site's **Settings** and find **Ask AI**. Only Docs Studio admins can change it.

### Switch it on
Turn the switch **On**.

### Publish
Readers get the assistant after the next [publish](/docs-studio/publishing). Turning it off works the same way.
::::

![The Ask AI card: switched On, this month's answers, Name readers see "Ask Acme", and three suggested questions.](/docs-assets/docs-studio/ask-ai/settings-ask-ai.webp)

## Make it yours

- **Name readers see**: what the button and panel are called, like "Ask Acme". Empty means **Ask AI**.
- **Suggested questions**: up to 5, shown before someone types and on the site's home page. Type one and press Enter; choose **×** on a question to remove it.

## What readers get

- **Ask** from the header button, the search box, or the home page.
- Answers come only from your published docs. Each claim has a numbered source that opens the section it came from.
- On a page, they can ask about that page or one section of it.

## Answers each month

Each site has a monthly number of Ask AI answers while Docs Studio is in beta. The **Ask AI** card shows how many have been used this month. At the limit, readers get links to ask Claude or ChatGPT with the page attached instead.

Better pages make better answers: clear headings, one topic per section, and the words your readers use. See [Write in the editor](/docs-studio/editor).
