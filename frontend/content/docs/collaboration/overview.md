Pages and Markdown documents are where your team discusses the work, not just where it's written. Comment on a sentence, a block or the whole page, suggest the exact words, bring in the people who need to answer, and keep the Jira issues next to the text they're about. The page's text never changes until someone who can edit it says yes.

## How it fits together

1. **Someone comments.** Select a few words and choose **Comment**, or press **⌘⌥M**. The passage is highlighted for everyone who can see comments. See [Comments](/collaboration/comments).
2. **The right people hear about it.** @mention someone or assign the thread and they're told under the bell, by email, on their phone or in Slack. See [Notifications](/collaboration/notifications).
3. **The discussion reaches a decision.** Suggest the words, or let AI draft them from the thread, and an editor accepts them into the page as a new version. See [Suggested edits and AI help](/collaboration/suggested-edits).
4. **Work that's bigger than a comment goes to Jira.** Create an issue from the thread; it links back, and resolving one offers to close the other. See [Jira in pages](/collaboration/jira-in-pages).
5. **Nothing falls through.** Home lists the threads waiting on you, and a sign-off says when threads are still open. See [Assign, Home and sign-off](/collaboration/assign-and-sign-off).

## Where it works

| Item | Comments | Jira chips, cards and lists |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pages | Yes | Yes |
| Markdown documents with an owner | Yes | Chips and links |
| Files (PDFs, slides, sheets, video, HTML) | Not yet | No |
| End-to-end encrypted documents | Never (Markdrop can't read them) | No |
| Documents published without an account | No | No |

Commenting is for people who are signed in and can open the item. Readers who aren't signed in see a clean page, unless the owner chose to [show comments publicly](/collaboration/settings-and-privacy#show-comments-publicly).
