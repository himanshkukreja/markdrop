## Ask your team

**Ask** *team* in the sidebar answers from that team's pages, meetings and memory, and nothing else: other teams never mix in, even open ones.

Switch on **Ask the whole company** to ask across everything you can read in the workspace: your teams, open teams, company pages and every team's weekly brief. Answers never use a private team you're not in (admins excepted). Switch it off to go back to your team.

::clip{src="/docs-assets/teams/ask-and-search/ask-toggle" alt="The Ask page on Ask Sales, with team chips for Sales and Marketing. Riya turns on Ask the whole company and the title changes to Ask Acme, then turns it off again."}

The chips under the title switch between your teams.

## Search

On **Search**, choose a workspace under **Where**, then **Team**:

- **All teams**: everything you can open in the workspace.
- **Company-wide only**: pages in no team.
- A team: only that team's.

![Search for "Q4" in Acme with the Team filter open: All teams, Company-wide only, Sales, Marketing, Engineering. Each result shows its team after the workspace, like "Acme › Sales".](/docs-assets/teams/ask-and-search/search-filter.webp)

Each result shows its team after the workspace name. A private team's pages show up only for its members and admins.
