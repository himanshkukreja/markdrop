A workspace is your company: one sign-in, one member list, one set of AI limits. **Teams** split it into spaces, so Sales, Marketing and Engineering each get their own pages, meetings inbox, memory and Ask, without a second workspace. People can be in several teams, and the teams stay connected: every team publishes a weekly brief, a page can belong to more than one team, and projects bring several teams together.

## The sidebar

Open a workspace in the sidebar and you see two parts:

- **Company**: **Home** (your teams' briefs, company pages and meetings), **Pages** (company-wide pages and folders), **Meetings** and **People**.
- **Your teams**: each team you're in opens to its **Home**, **Pages**, **Meetings**, **Memory** and **Ask** *team*, with its projects under it. **Browse teams** lists every team in the workspace.

## Who sees what

| | Members of the team | Others in the workspace | Workspace admins |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Open team**: pages, meetings, memory | Read and edit, as the item allows | Read (browse and search it); it never mixes into their own teams' views or answers | Everything |
| **Private team**: pages, meetings, memory | Read and edit, as the item allows | Its name and weekly brief only | Everything |
| **Weekly brief** | Yes; leads edit it | Yes | Yes |
| **Company pages** (in no team) | Yes | Yes | Yes |

A team decides who can *browse* its content. Sharing still works the way it always did: a page you share with a person or by link opens for them, even in a private team. See [Share across teams and projects](/teams/share-and-projects#private-teams-and-sharing).

> [!NOTE]
> Workspace admins can see every team's content, private teams included. A private team's settings say so.

## Good to know

- Nothing moved when Teams arrived. Everything you had stays company-wide until someone puts it in a team. See [Set up teams](/teams/set-up).
- AI limits stay workspace-wide: teams share them.
- Teams work from AI apps too. See [Teams for AI apps](/teams/for-ai-apps).
