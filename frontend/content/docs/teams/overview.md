A workspace is your company: one sign-in, one member list, one set of AI limits. **Teams** split it into spaces, so Sales, Marketing and Engineering each get their own pages, meetings inbox, memory and Ask, without a second workspace. People can be in several teams, and the teams stay connected: every team publishes a weekly brief, a page can belong to more than one team, and projects bring several teams together.

Every workspace starts with one team, **General**: everyone is in it, and it holds what's for the whole company (the handbook, all-hands, policies). New people join it on their own.

## The sidebar

The sidebar shows one workspace at a time. From the top:

- **The workspace switcher**: the workspace's name and how many people are in it. Choose it to switch workspace, make a new one or open its settings.
- **Search** (⌘K), **Home**, **Inbox** and **Ask** *workspace*.
- **Favorites**: pages, files, teams and projects you starred. Choose **⋯** on any of them in the sidebar, then **Add to favorites**.
- **Teams**: **General** first (marked **Everyone**), then the teams you're in. Choose a team to open its home; open the arrow to see its **Meetings**, **People & memory**, its projects, and its pages and files as a tree. **+** next to Teams creates a team (admins), and **Browse teams** lists the ones you're not in.
- **You**: **My files**, **Shared with me**, **Recent**, **Analytics** and **Trash**.

![The sidebar of the Acme workspace: the Acme switcher, Search, Home, Inbox and Ask Acme, then Teams with General (Everyone) and Sales open to Meetings, People & memory, Q4 launch and its pages, then Browse teams.](/docs-assets/teams/overview/sidebar.webp)

The sidebar remembers which teams and folders you left open, and follows the page you're on. Arrow keys move through it and Enter opens a row. On a phone the same tree is in the menu.

![Acme on a phone with the menu open: the same switcher, Teams tree and You section.](/docs-assets/teams/overview/phone.webp)

## General, the team everyone is in

General's home is the company's front door: its weekly brief, **Needs you**, **Across teams** (one line from each team's latest brief; a private team you're not in shows only its name), meetings, recently updated pages and files, **What's happening** across every team you can see, projects and who's new this month.

![General's home in Acme: the brief, Needs you, Across teams with a line from Sales, Marketing and Engineering, meetings, recently updated pages and files with thumbnails, and What's happening.](/docs-assets/teams/overview/general.webp)

- Everything that isn't in a team is in General, so everyone in the workspace can find it.
- Admins can rename it and change its icon, colour and description. It's always open, and it can't be deleted.
- You can't leave General or take someone out of it: leaving the workspace does that.

## Who sees what

| | Members of the team | Others in the workspace | Workspace admins |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Open team**: pages, meetings, memory | Read and edit, as the item allows | Read (browse and search it); it never mixes into their own teams' views or answers | Everything |
| **Private team**: pages, meetings, memory | Read and edit, as the item allows | Its name and weekly brief only | Everything |
| **Weekly brief** | Yes; leads edit it | Yes | Yes |
| **General** (in no other team) | Yes | Yes | Yes |

A team decides who can *browse* its content. Sharing still works the way it always did: a page you share with a person or by link opens for them, even in a private team. See [Share across teams and projects](/teams/share-and-projects#private-teams-and-sharing).

> [!NOTE]
> Workspace admins can see every team's content, private teams included. A private team's settings say so.

## Good to know

- Nothing moved when Teams arrived. Everything you had is in General until someone puts it in a team. See [Set up teams](/teams/set-up).
- AI limits stay workspace-wide: teams share them.
- Teams work from AI apps too. See [Teams for AI apps](/teams/for-ai-apps).
