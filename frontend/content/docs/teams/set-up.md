Everything in your workspace stays **company-wide** when you start using teams: nobody loses access to anything, and nothing changes until an admin says so.

## 1. Create your teams

Admins open **Browse teams** and choose **New team** for each group that works together. See [Create and manage teams](/teams/create-and-manage).

If your workspace has [Team groups](/workspaces/team#groups), they're offered under **Suggested from your groups**: choose **Make it a team** to start one with the same people, or **Dismiss**.

## 2. Sort existing content into teams

Admins see **Sort content into teams** in the sidebar until they finish it. It suggests where things go:

- **Meetings**: by who attended, and the team earlier meetings of the series went to.
- **Pages**: by who wrote them, or the folder they're in.
- **Folders**: by who files in them.

Each row starts on its suggestion. Change the team, choose **Keep company-wide**, or untick it. Nothing moves until you choose **Apply**; the count next to it says how many items will move. Choose **Finish without moving** when you're done, and the suggestions go away.

![Sort content into teams: Pages tab with rows like "Q4 pricing playbook, Riya Sharma, from who wrote it" set to Sales, and Apply 4 at the bottom.](/docs-assets/teams/set-up/review.webp)

## 3. Tell people

Members see **Your teams** in the sidebar as soon as they're added. Point them at **Browse teams** to join the open ones.
