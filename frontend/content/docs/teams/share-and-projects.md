Every page, document and file in a workspace is either in **General** (the team everyone is in) or in one or more other teams. A team's pages and files are in its **Files & pages**, from the sidebar.

![The Sales team's Files & pages: a search box, then the Enablement, Playbooks and Proposals folders (two of them open to their pages and PDFs), the team's own pages and a spreadsheet, and Archive at the end with one archived folder.](/docs-assets/teams/share-and-projects/files-and-pages.webp)

## Put something in a team

- **New page in a team**: choose **+** next to the team in the sidebar, or **New page** on its home or its **Files & pages**.
- **Move or share an existing item**: open it, choose **Share**, and under **Teams** choose **Add to a team**. An item can be in several teams: each sees it in its own space. Choose **×** on a team to take it out; with no teams left, it's back in General.

::clip{src="/docs-assets/teams/share-and-projects/share-flow" alt="Alex opens Company holidays 2026, chooses Share, then Add to a team under Teams, and picks Engineering. Engineering appears as a chip under Teams."}

Sub-pages follow their page. To add something to a team you need to be able to edit it and be in that team (admins can add to any team).

## Folders

A folder can belong to a team too: things filed into it join that team unless they're in one already. Admins and the folder's owner set this.

### Move a folder to another team

In a team's **Files & pages**, open the **⋯** menu on a folder and choose **Move to team…**, then pick the team. The folder moves with everything in it: its subfolders, pages and their sub-pages, documents and files. Before anything changes, the dialog shows what moves and what happens to who can open it:

- **Visible to the whole workspace** becomes **visible to the new team only**. If the new team is open, everyone in the workspace can still view it, as with any open team's pages.
- **Private** items stay private.
- People an item was **shared with by name** keep it.
- **Public links keep working**, because customers or prospects may have them. The dialog lists them, so you can change any you no longer want public.
- The folder's [access settings](/workspaces/folder-access) move with it. Access given to the old team now goes to the new one. If the folder was restricted by a folder it sat in, it keeps that restriction.

If the folder's parent stays in the old team, the folder moves to the top of the workspace. Its old addresses on your domain redirect. A big folder finishes in the background: the dialog shows the progress and you can close it. The move is listed, with who made it, in the folder's **Recent changes** (**⋯ › Who can access…**).

::clip{src="/docs-assets/teams/share-and-projects/move-folder" alt="In Sales's Files & pages, Riya opens the menu on the Enablement folder and chooses Move to team…. She chooses Move to Marketing; the folder leaves Sales's Files & pages and a message says Enablement moved to Marketing."}

Workspace admins can move any folder. A team lead can move a folder of their team to another team they're in. Meetings folders and deal folders stay where they are.

## Private teams and sharing

A private team decides who can **find** its content: its pages don't appear in anyone else's sidebar, lists, search, Ask answers or memory. It doesn't undo a share you make on purpose:

- Shared with a person: they can open it.
- **Anyone with the link**: anyone with the link can open it.
- **Only people added**: only you and the people you added, as before.

The Share dialog reminds you when an item is in a private team.

## Projects

A project is a space that several teams work on together, like "Q4 launch": its own pages and meetings, and the memory of every team in it. It appears on the home of each of its teams, and members of those teams can all contribute.

![The Q4 launch project in Acme, a project of Sales, Marketing and Engineering: its pages and meetings, the memory lines from its teams, and its teams and people.](/docs-assets/teams/share-and-projects/project.webp)

Admins and the leads of a team create a project from the team's home (**Projects › New project**). Choose its teams, and add people from outside them if you need to. Put pages in it from their **Share** dialog, like a team.
