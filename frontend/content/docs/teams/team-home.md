Open a team from **Your teams** in the sidebar, or from **Browse teams**. Its home has everything the team is working on in one place:

- **Weekly brief**: what the team shipped, decided, is blocked on and is focusing on.
- **Recent pages** and **Meetings**: the newest in the team's space, with links to all of them.
- **Memory**: who's doing what, from the team's meetings and pages.
- **Projects**: the cross-team projects the team is part of.
- **Jira**: open, overdue and recently done issues in the team's Jira projects.
- **Members**, with the team's leads marked.

![The Sales team's weekly brief, "Week of Nov 10": a shipped line, a decision, a blocker and a focus, each with its icon, and Earlier and Re-draft above them.](/docs-assets/teams/team-home/brief.webp)

## The weekly brief

Every Monday morning each team gets a short brief of the week before, drawn from the team's pages and meetings that every member can open (never a private page or a meeting kept to its attendees). It publishes on its own: no one has to send it.

The brief is how teams stay connected without mixing their content. **Everyone in the workspace can read every team's brief**, private teams included, and Ask can use the briefs when you ask the whole company.

Leads and workspace admins can change the brief at any time:

- **Edit a line**: hover over it and choose the pencil.
- **Hide a line**: choose the eye. It disappears for everyone else; you still see it, crossed out, and can show it again.
- **Add a line**: **Add a line** at the bottom.
- **Re-draft**: writes this week's brief again from what's there now.

::clip{src="/docs-assets/teams/team-home/brief-edit" alt="Riya edits the blocker line in Sales's brief to say Engineering ships SSO on Thursday, saves it, then hides the focus line, which turns grey and crossed out for her."}

**Earlier** opens the briefs of previous weeks. A team's settings can also send the brief to its members by email or Slack when it publishes. See [Create and manage teams](/teams/create-and-manage#slack-jira-and-the-weekly-digest).

## A private team you're not in

You see its name, what it's for and its weekly brief, nothing else. To join, ask one of its leads to add you.

![A private Sales team seen by someone outside it: "This is a private team. Only its members see its pages, meetings and memory." above Sales's weekly brief.](/docs-assets/teams/team-home/private-outsider.webp)

## Open teams

Anyone in the workspace can open an open team's home, pages and meetings, and choose **Join team** to become a member.
