Something isn't working, or you have a question? Write to **[himanshu@markdrop.in](mailto:himanshu@markdrop.in)**. A person reads every message, and you'll get an answer within two business days, usually sooner.

## What to include

- What you were doing and what happened instead.
- The link to the document, page, file or workspace, if there is one.
- The email address of your Markdrop account.
- A screenshot, if it helps.

Never send passwords, Zoom or Slack secrets, or encryption keys. We never ask for them.

## Start here

- [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting) answers the most common problems.
- [Frequently asked questions](/help/faq) covers accounts, sharing, encryption and your data.

## Integrations

| Integration | Setup and fixes | Disconnect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zoom | [Zoom](/integrations/zoom): connect, the Zoom card's states, meetings without a transcript | [Removing the app](/integrations/zoom#removing-the-app) |
| Slack | [Slack](/integrations/slack) | In the Slack card, or in Slack's app settings |
| Jira | [Jira](/integrations/jira) | In the Jira card, or in your Atlassian account's connected apps |

For the **Markdrop app for Zoom**, include your Zoom account's name or the email of the Zoom admin who approved the app, and the topic and date of a meeting that didn't come in. The Zoom card's **Latest from Zoom** list shows each meeting's state and reason.

## Privacy and data requests

To ask what Markdrop holds about you, or to have it deleted, write to the same address. The [privacy policy](https://markdrop.in/privacy) says what we keep and for how long, including [what Markdrop does with Zoom data](https://markdrop.in/privacy#zoom).

## Security

If you found a security problem, write to the same address with "Security" in the subject. Please give us a chance to fix it before telling anyone else.
