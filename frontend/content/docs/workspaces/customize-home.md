Your **Home** is the first screen after you sign in. The greeting and the Ask bar are always at the top. Under them are two columns of widgets that you choose: a wide **main** column and a narrow **side** column. Everyone starts with the same Default Home. You can change yours without changing anyone else's.

## Change your Home

1. Click **Customize** at the top right of your Home. A bar opens with **Templates**, **Reset to default** and **Done**, and each widget gets its own buttons.
2. Arrange the widgets:
   - **↑** and **↓** move a widget up or down in its column.
   - **Move to side** and **Move to main** move it to the other column.
   - **⚙** opens its settings, such as which team or folder it shows and how many items.
   - **✕** removes it.
3. To add one, click **Add widget** (or **Add widget to the side**) and pick it from the list. It goes at the top of that column. A widget that needs a team, a folder or a page asks you to choose one.
4. Click **Done**.

Every change is saved as you make it. You don't drag widgets: the buttons do the moving, so this also works on a phone.

![Customize mode: the bar with Templates, Reset to default, Workspace default and Done, Add widget buttons above each column, and move, settings and remove buttons on each widget](/docs-assets/workspaces/customize-home/edit.webp)

A Home can have up to 16 widgets. On a phone or a narrow window, the two columns become one: the main column first, then the side column.

## Widgets

| Widget | What it shows | Settings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Needs you** | Today's meetings, mentions, sign-off and access requests, and comments waiting on you. | How many |
| **My to-dos** | Open action items that @mention you, from meeting notes and any page. Tick them here. | How many |
| **Team backlog** | A team's [backlog](/teams/backlog), such as its open follow-ups or bugs. | Team, category, status, how many |
| **Recent pages & files** | What you opened last, anywhere. In the main column they're cards with previews; on the side, a list. | How many |
| **Favorites** | The pages, files, teams and projects you starred in this workspace. | How many |
| **A folder** | The newest items in one folder. | Folder, how many |
| **Meetings** | The latest meetings, from every team or from one team. | Team, how many |
| **Team brief** | A team's weekly brief. | Team |
| **Who's doing what** | A team's [memory](/teams/meetings-and-memory): who is working on what. | Team |
| **Deals** | Open HubSpot deals with their stage and amount. Only in a workspace with [HubSpot](/integrations/hubspot) connected. | How many |
| **What changed** | What happened since you last looked at the workspace or one team: meetings and their decisions, new and updated pages and files, action items, comments. See [What changed](/workspaces/team#what-changed). | Team, how many |
| **Activity** | New pages, files and meetings across the teams you can see. | How many |
| **My documents' views** | Views and readers of what you published, and your top links. | Last 7 or 30 days |
| **Pinned page** | One page you choose, shown right on your Home to read. | Page |
| **Quick actions** | New page, upload a file, import a meeting, search. | |
| **Ask suggestions** | Questions your team asks, ready to ask again. | |
| **Workspaces** | Your workspaces with their people, items and meetings. | |

A widget only ever shows what you can open. If you leave a team or a folder is deleted, its widget says it isn't available and offers **Remove**.

## Start from a template

In Customize, open **Templates** and pick one. It replaces your current layout, and **Undo** in the message brings it back.

| Template | Main column | Side column |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Default** | Recent pages & files, Meetings, Workspaces | Needs you, My to-dos |
| **Writer** | Recent pages & files, your newest page pinned | Favorites, Quick actions |
| **Meetings first** | Meetings, Who's doing what | My to-dos, Needs you, Ask suggestions |
| **Sales** | Deals, your sales team's meetings, its backlog's follow-ups | My to-dos, Needs you |
| **Manager** | Team brief, Who's doing what, Activity | Needs you, Team backlog |

Templates use your own first team, or the team linked to HubSpot for Sales. Widgets that don't apply to you, such as Deals without HubSpot, are left out.

**Reset to default** forgets your layout. You see your workspace's default Home again, or Default if it has none.

## Set a default for your workspace

Workspace owners and admins can choose the Home that members see until they customize their own:

1. Arrange your Home the way you want members to see it.
2. In Customize, open **Workspace default** and click **Set as workspace default**.

Members who already customized their Home keep theirs. A widget for a team that a member can't open isn't shown to them. Team widgets added from a template show each member their own team. **Clear workspace default** in the same menu goes back to Default.

## Each workspace has its own Home

Your Home follows the workspace the sidebar shows. A different workspace has its own layout, with its own teams, folders and favorites.

## From your AI assistant

The [MCP tools](/ai/tools) `get_dashboard` and `set_dashboard` read and change your Home. For example: "Put my Sales meetings and the Sales backlog's follow-ups on my Markdrop Home" or "Reset my Home to the default".
