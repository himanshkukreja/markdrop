---
title: Share a dashboard
description: Share a read-only link, or embed a dashboard in your own app.
icon: share
---

Choose **Share** on a dashboard. A link opens it read-only; people need no Acme account.

<Frame caption="The Share dialog with a read-only link.">
  <img src="/images/share.png" alt="The Share dialog: a read-only link with a copy button and an expiry date." />
</Frame>

To embed it, copy the **Embed** code into your page. Embedded dashboards follow your site's light or dark mode.
