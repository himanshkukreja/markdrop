(Settings › Integrations and Workspace settings › Integrations). They are trademarks of their
owners, used only to name each integration. Their use implies no endorsement of Markdrop.

Shapes and colours are unmodified. Editor metadata and fixed sizes were stripped; `*-white.svg` is the
owner's single-colour mark in white, shown in the dark theme where the colour mark lacks contrast.
